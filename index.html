Help and privacy

Play a tone or run a sound check

Use the iPhone speaker and start at a low volume. Keep the speaker away from your ear during the low-frequency tone. Tap Stop at any time. The countdown shows playback time. The app cannot detect liquid or assess damage.

If playback is blocked, choose iPhone from the output picker or Control Center, or disconnect the external device. Earpiece and left/right checks require a supported route. If both channels sound centered, check Mono Audio under Settings › Accessibility › Audio & Visual.

If your iPhone got wet

Follow Apple’s drying guidance. If you see a liquid detection alert, unplug the cable and let the connector dry before attempting to charge again.

App Store download

A one-time App Store payment includes all features, including advanced tones, saved presets, and listening comparisons. There are no in-app purchases, ads, or subscriptions.

Privacy policy

There is no account, microphone recording, analytics, advertising or developer server. The app produces audio on the device and stores presets, comparisons and progress locally. Apple handles the download payment through the App Store. You choose where to send any image, preset or listening record you share. You can remove local records and settings by deleting the app.

使用說明與隱私

播放輔助音或做聽感自檢

使用 iPhone 揚聲器，從低音量開始。播放低頻輔助音時請勿將揚聲器貼近耳朵。你可以隨時停止。倒數只顯示播放時間，App 無法偵測液體或判斷裝置損壞。

播放受阻時，在輸出選擇器或控制中心選擇 iPhone，或中斷外接裝置。聽筒與左右聲道試聽需要對應的輸出能力。如果兩側聽起來都在中央，請到「設定 › 輔助使用 › 音訊與視覺」檢查單聲道音訊。

裝置沾水時

遵循 Apple 的乾燥指引。如出現 液體偵測提示，請先拔下連接線，等接頭乾燥後再嘗試充電。

App Store 下載

在 App Store 一次付費即可使用全部功能，包括進階音型、多組預設和聽感對照。無 App 內購買、無廣告、無訂閱。

隱私權政策

App 無帳號、麥克風錄音、統計、廣告或開發者伺服器。聲音在裝置上產生，預設、對照和進度儲存在裝置上。下載費用由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。分享圖片、預設或聽感紀錄時，由你選擇接收方。刪除 App 可移除本機紀錄與設定。
